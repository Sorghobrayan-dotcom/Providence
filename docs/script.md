# The script

Read it straight down. **Bold** is what you say. *Italic* is what you do on
screen while you say it. A line break is a breath — take it.

2m51 at a measured pace. Record in three takes, one per part.

---

## Part one — the gap

*The site, still. Do not touch anything.*

> **Today, our engines simulate gravity with extraordinary precision.**
>
> **Every collision.**
> **Every arrow.**
> **Every falling body.**
>
> **But ask them one question.**
> **What does it cost to betray someone?**
>
> **Nothing.**
>
> **To a game engine, a murder and a handshake are simply two different state
> changes.**
> **The body falls.**
> **The world moves on.**
> **It remembers nothing.**

*Now scroll slowly down the library on the left, so the names go past.*

> **So we asked ourselves...**
>
> **What if consequences had laws, just like gravity?**
> **What if relationships had physics?**
> **What if morality could become part of the simulation itself?**
>
> **That question became...**
>
> **Providence.**
>
> **Not a game.**
> **Not a game engine.**
> **A library that lives beneath one.**

## Part two — the Bible, read differently

*Stay on the library. The 24 names are on screen. Slow down here.*

> **It is one of humanity's deepest explorations of human nature.**
>
> **Its pages are filled with fear.**
> **Courage.**
> **Betrayal.**
> **Mercy.**
> **Sacrifice.**
> **Forgiveness.**
> **Relationships that shape destinies.**

> **Providence doesn't copy those stories.**

*Click* **Le Serment Brise**. *Walk up to him. Press* **E**. *Choose*
*« Demande-lui de te suivre. »*

> **Jonah may run from the mission you just gave him.**
> **Peter may deny you when fear becomes stronger than loyalty.**

*Toggle* **under threat**. **Now stop talking for eight seconds.** Let the fear
meter climb. This silence is the best moment in the video — do not fill it.

*He denies you, on his own.*

> **Martha and Mary may witness the same interruption...**
> **yet choose completely different responses.**

*Toggle it off. Press* **K** *three times. He comes back.*

*Open* **Relations**. *Click* **betray peter**.

> **A blessing can travel.**
> **A debt can be transferred.**
> **Grace cannot be purchased.**

*He turns and walks away.*

> **And the world remembers.**

## While recording

Read slower than feels natural. Slow and clear beats fast and native.

If you stumble on a word, keep going. You will cut it in Clipchamp, and one
retake of a sentence is twenty seconds.

The eleven words worth rehearsing twice: **PROV**-i-dence, **SKRIP**-cher,
bi-**TRAY**, **GRAV**-i-ty, **JOH**-nuh (not Jonas), **PEE**-ter, **MAR**-thuh,
**GRAYSS**, you-**VER**-zhun, **CON**-si-kwen-ses, **DEE**-pist.

Put the key sentences on screen as text while you say them. A word a judge does
not catch then costs nothing.

## If you run long

Cut from part three, never from parts two or four. Drop the three kindnesses and
go straight from the denial to the betrayal — fifteen seconds, and the betrayal
is the stronger beat anyway.
